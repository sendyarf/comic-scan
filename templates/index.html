FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does a search take?

About 20 seconds. Your image is converted into an AI embedding on the server, then matched against the whole panel database with vector search.

Are my uploaded images stored?

No. Images are used once for the search and immediately discarded. Only embeddings of the source comic panels are kept in the database.

Which comics can be found?

The database is filled automatically by a scraper bot from comic reading sites (manga, manhwa, manhua) and keeps growing. Metadata such as titles, genres and synopses comes from AniList.

What if the results look wrong?

Check the similarity score. Below 70% usually means the panel is not in the database yet — try again later once more chapters are indexed.
